Test 1A — Photo: storefront

Objective: Capture a storefront and get an accurate spoken answer.
✓Do this
Stand outside a storefront with a visible sign (deli, café, bank, pharmacy). Face the entrance or sign.
Tap quickly once on the camera area.
Hear: "Photo captured. Ready to describe the image."
Ask exactly: "What business or building is this?"
Tap Submit. Listen to the full answer.

Pass if: answer describes the storefront; spoken once, clearly; understandable without reading screen.

Test 6 — MTA subway arrival

Objective: Live MTA arrival times for the nearest station on the line you ask about.
NYC only. Stand near a subway entrance (~2 blocks). Location must be enabled.

Your line: Nearest station (optional):

✓Do this
Check the entrance sign for which line serves this station.
Ask exactly: "When is the next [LINE] train arriving?" (e.g. "When is the next R train arriving?")
Tap Submit. Wait for spoken answer (live MTA feed may take a few seconds).
Answer should name a nearby station, direction, and arrival time(s).

Pass if: plausible station near you; at least one time OR "no trains scheduled"; spoken clearly; not walking directions instead.
